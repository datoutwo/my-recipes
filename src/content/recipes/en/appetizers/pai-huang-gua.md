---
title: Smashed Cucumber Salad
key: pai-huang-gua
language: en
description: Cracked cucumbers are tossed with garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, and sesame oil, then chilled overnight to absorb the flavors.
image: /my-recipes/images/recipes/pai-huang-gua.jpg
category: Appetizers
tags: [cucumber, chilled salad, appetizer]
prepTime: 30
cookTime: 0
servings: 6
difficulty: Easy
ingredients:
  - 2 cucumbers
  - 3–5 garlic cloves
  - ½–1 chili
  - ½–1 tbsp sesame oil
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp salt, for drawing water from the cucumbers and then rinsed off
  - ½–1 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp white vinegar
  - 1 tsp black pepper
  - Spicy Sichuan peppercorn sauce or chili oil, optional
instructions:
  - Rinse the cucumbers with hot water and drain.
  - Make lengthwise cuts to mark six sections along each cucumber, without cutting all the way through, so it stays connected.
  - Smack the cucumbers to crack them, then cut into bite-sized pieces.
  - Add the salt and toss. Leave for 20 minutes to draw out moisture.
  - While the cucumbers rest, mince the garlic and dice the chili. Remove the chili seeds if desired.
  - Rinse the salt from the cucumbers and drain well.
  - Add the garlic, chili, sesame oil, soy sauce, sugar, white vinegar, and black pepper. Add spicy Sichuan peppercorn sauce or chili oil if desired, then toss to combine.
  - Transfer to a container or cover tightly with plastic wrap. Refrigerate overnight before serving.
