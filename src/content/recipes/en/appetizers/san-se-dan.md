---
title: Three-Color Egg
key: san-se-dan
language: en
description: Century egg, salted egg, and chicken egg are steamed in layers, chilled, and sliced to serve.
image: /my-recipes/images/recipes/san-se-dan.jpg
category: Appetizers
tags: [century egg, salted egg, steamed egg, appetizer]
prepTime: 15
cookTime: 45
servings: 4-5
difficulty: Easy
ingredients:
  - 5 chicken eggs, whites and yolks separated
  - 2 century eggs
  - 2 salted eggs
  - 1 tsp mirin or rice wine
  - 1½ cups water for the rice cooker, added in 1 cup and ½ cup portions
instructions:
  - Boil the century eggs for 15 minutes. Let them cool, then peel.
  - Separate the whites and yolks of the 5 chicken eggs.
  - Peel and dice the century eggs and salted eggs.
  - Stir the mirin or rice wine into the egg whites. Strain the whites if you like; this step is optional.
  - Put the diced century eggs, salted eggs, and egg whites in a steaming dish. Add 1 cup of water to the rice cooker and steam until it switches to warm, about 30 minutes.
  - After it switches to warm, pour the yolks over the top. Add ½ cup of water to the rice cooker and steam until it switches to warm again, about 15 minutes.
  - Let the steamed egg cool, then chill until set. Cut into 16 slices to serve.
notes:
  - Prepare the other ingredients while the century eggs boil for 15 minutes.
