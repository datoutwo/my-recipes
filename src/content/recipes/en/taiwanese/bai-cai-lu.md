title: Taiwanese Braised Napa Cabbage
key: bai-cai-lu
language: en
description: Napa cabbage is gently braised without added water in an enamel pot with dried shrimp, dried flatfish, mushrooms, and tofu skin.
image: /my-recipes/images/recipes/bai-cai-lu.jpg
category: Taiwanese
tags: [Napa cabbage, dried shrimp, dried flatfish, Taiwanese home cooking, waterless braise]
prepTime: 30
cookTime: 60
servings: '5-6'
difficulty: Medium
ingredients:
  - 1 head Napa cabbage (about 1.5 kg)
  - 1 handful dried shrimp
  - 5–6 shiitake mushrooms
  - 5 pieces dried flatfish (bian-yu)
  - 3 carrots
  - 1 pack tofu skin/yuba rolls
  - 6 garlic cloves
  - 1 handful wood ear mushrooms
  - Cooking oil for frying (amount not specified)
  - Salt for seasoning the cabbage (amount not specified)
instructions:
  - Soak the shiitake mushrooms and dried shrimp in water for 4 hours, and the wood ear mushrooms for 1 hour.
  - Wash and cut the Napa cabbage into pieces. Peel and roll-cut the carrots. Slice the shiitake and wood ear mushrooms into strips. Peel the garlic and cut the dried flatfish into small pieces.
  - Heat oil in a pan and fry the dried shrimp and flatfish until bubbling. Transfer them to an enamel pot.
  - Use the remaining oil to fry the garlic and carrots until fragrant, then transfer them to the pot.
  - Dry-fry the shiitake and wood ear mushrooms until fragrant, then add them to the pot.
  - Stir-fry the cabbage with oil and salt until it releases a little liquid and softens slightly. Add it to the pot.
  - Stir everything together and cover the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium and braise. Do not add water; the washed cabbage releases the cooking liquid.
  - Add the tofu skin/yuba rolls after about 30 minutes of braising.
  - Continue cooking until the total cooking time is about 45–60 minutes. Stir or turn the ingredients during cooking as needed, keeping the pot at a gentle simmer.
notes:
  - Hands-on preparation takes about 20–30 minutes. Soaking the shiitake and dried shrimp for 4 hours and the wood ear mushrooms for 1 hour is additional.
  - Total cooking time is about 45–60 minutes.
